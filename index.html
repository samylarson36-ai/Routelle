    Questions we hear a lot.

    How long does setup take?

    Most teams are dispatching their first route the same afternoon. If you can export a spreadsheet, you can start.

    Do my drivers need a special phone?

    No. The driver app runs on any recent iPhone or Android phone and works in the browser as a fallback.

    Can customers choose a delivery window?

    Yes. Share a booking link or import windows with your orders. Routelle plans around them.

    What happens if I cancel?

    You can export every order, route and proof of delivery as CSV or PDF whenever you like.
